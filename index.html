What I Do

Senior DevOps & Platform Engineer with 10+ years building, scaling, and automating cloud-native platforms across AWS, GCP, and Azure. I design and operate large-scale Kubernetes platforms, build reusable infrastructure as code, and make software delivery fast, observable, and secure.

I've architected and operated production platforms for financial institutions, telcos, airlines, and fintechs — including a Platform-as-a-Service on AWS EKS hosting trading, clearing, and market-data workloads at a commodity futures exchange and clearinghouse.

Across India, Germany, and Singapore, my work spans Kubernetes (EKS/GKE), Terraform, CI/CD, multi-cloud networking, observability, and DevSecOps.

How I Do It

I lean on disciplined engineering: infrastructure as code with Terraform, GitOps with ArgoCD, declarative observability, and automation that removes toil. I've led an observability migration from Prometheus + Thanos + Tempo to the Elastic Stack, cut cloud spend by ~35% through automation, and reduced environment setup time by ~40% with reusable Terraform modules.

As a CNCF Kubestronaut (CKA, CKAD, CKS, KCNA, KCSA), I care deeply about running Kubernetes securely and reliably in regulated, financial-grade environments — from network firewalls and segmentation to secrets management and CIS benchmarks.

Author of: CKA Book · AI-LLM Learning Hub
